# Custos AI — Institutional Partnership Brief

Custos seeks to work with enterprises and public institutions as an independent technology partner, not merely as a one-off vendor or general-purpose subcontractor.

## Potential engagement models

1. Strategic research partnerships with defined questions, test environments, milestones, evidence, confidentiality, and IP allocation.
2. Technology licensing with defined field of use, term, permitted modification, sublicensing, security, support, and termination.
3. Deployment and integration agreements with scoped responsibilities, customer dependencies, acceptance tests, and operational limits.
4. Joint evaluation programs with agreed threat models, reproducible tests, independent review options, and residual-risk records.
5. Continuing technical partnerships for updates, reviews, support, and architecture evolution.

## Intellectual property

- Background IP: technology and know-how that pre-existed the engagement.
- Foreground IP: deliverables and inventions created under the engagement.
- Improvements: modifications or derivative developments to existing technology.
- Customer data: data supplied by or generated within the customer's environment.

Ownership, license-back, reuse, derivative works, and data rights must be negotiated explicitly. These are intended engagement structures, not claims of signed contracts, public-sector awards, or revenue.
