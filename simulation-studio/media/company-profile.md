# Custos AI — Company Profile

**Positioning:** An independent technology venture researching architectures for preserving meaningful human agency around increasingly autonomous AI.

**Tagline:** Preserving Humanity's Agency.

## Mission

Custos does not assume that AI can be controlled perfectly. It researches architectures that seek to maximize the time, probability, and recoverability of humanity's meaningful authority over increasingly autonomous AI systems.

## Business direction

Custos intends to engage enterprises and public institutions as an independent technology partner through negotiated B2B and B2G relationships. Potential structures include research partnerships, licensing, scoped integration, joint evaluation, and continuing technical agreements. These are intended models, not existing contracts.

## Current status

Early-stage research and prototype effort. Current implementation uses local services and mock execution adapters. No customer deployment, production certification, independent security audit, or guarantee of complete AI control is claimed.
