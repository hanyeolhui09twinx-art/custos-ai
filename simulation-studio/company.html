Custos is an early-stage technology venture in formation, being developed around core research, independent technology assets, accountable partnerships, and long-term technical progress.

FOUNDER'S NOTE

What matters is not only what AI can do, but what humanity remains able to decide.

The founder's role is not to implement every technology alone. It is to define the problem precisely, establish architectural principles, assemble the talent and resources required, and remain accountable for the company's direction against evidence.

COMPANY DESIGN

Independence must be protected in both organization and contracts.

01 / MISSION

Purpose before product

Product sales are a means to sustain and realize the mission and research, not the mission itself.

02 / TECHNOLOGY

Independent core IP

Protect core technology and background IP through explicit contract terms while respecting customer rights.

03 / PARTNERSHIPS

Independent partner

Enterprise and public-sector relationships are based on agreements defining technology, responsibility, IP, and evidence.

04 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Independent scrutiny

Technical safety claims require independent challenge and reproducible evidence.
