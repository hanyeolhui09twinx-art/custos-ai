# Day 016 — Human Agency Preservation Corporate Platform

**Recorded:** 03 November 2026  
**Scope:** Corporate positioning, public website architecture, institutional partnership model, brand media, and browser-level validation.

## Mission and company positioning

Reframed the corporate website around Custos's highest-level objective: preserve meaningful human agency around increasingly autonomous AI. The message explicitly rejects a guarantee of perfect control and instead focuses on maximizing:

- Time for humans to understand and decide.
- The probability that legitimate intervention can take effect.
- Containment when a control point fails.
- Recovery paths after safeguards are compromised.
- Integrity of authority and oversight.

The mission page describes these as research objectives, not capabilities already proven.

## Corporate website

Added dedicated Mission, Institutional Partnerships, and Media Room pages, and rewrote Company and Contact pages. Reworked the home page to place human agency preservation above the product feature description. Preserved the existing Simulation Lab, Local Control Room, technical architecture, security, research, and developer documentation.

Updated desktop and mobile navigation, restored the Security & Trust top-level entry, retained the Local Control Room CTA, and kept Terms, responsible disclosure, and structured contact intake links.

## B2B and B2G business model

Described intended institutional relationships as contract-defined partnerships rather than one-off item delivery or undifferentiated subcontracting. The brief covers strategic research, licensing, scoped integration, joint evaluation, and continuing technical agreements.

Separated Background IP, Foreground IP, Improvements, and Customer Data. The pages state that ownership, licenses, reuse, responsibilities, and exit conditions must be negotiated per agreement. No existing customer contract, government award, signed partnership, or revenue is claimed.
